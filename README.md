# COFFEE-SHOP-SALES-ANALYSIS
☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard:

📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data of a Coffee Shop to uncover actionable business insights that can improve operational efficiency, customer experience, and overall sales performance.

The dashboard was created using Microsoft Excel with the help of:
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Slicers
KPI Cards
Data Cleaning & Transformation
Interactive Dashboard Design

The analysis helps identify:
Peak sales hours
Best-selling products
Store-wise performance
Customer ordering behavior
Category-wise sales contribution
🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales data and generate meaningful insights that can help improve business decision-making and increase profitability.

KPI Metrics
💰 Total Sales Revenue
👣 Total Footfall
🧾 Average Bill Per Person
☕ Average Orders Per Person
🔍 Recommended Analysis Performed

1. Sales Analysis by Day & Hour
Analyzed how sales vary across weekdays and different hours of the day
Identified peak business hours for customer activity

Insight:
Morning and afternoon hours generated the highest number of orders.

3. Monthly Revenue Analysis
Calculated total sales revenue for each month
Compared month-on-month sales performance

Insight:
Sales showed noticeable growth across months, indicating increasing customer demand.

4. Store Location Performance
Compared footfall and sales across different store locations:
Astoria
Hell’s Kitchen
Lower Manhattan

Insight:
Hell’s Kitchen generated the highest sales revenue among all stores.

5. Product Performance Analysis
Identified top-selling products based on:
Quantity sold
Revenue generated

Insight:
Barista Espresso and Brewed Chai Tea were among the highest revenue-generating products.

6. Product Category Analysis
Analyzed contribution of different product categories to total sales
Categories Included:
Coffee
Tea
Bakery
Drinking Chocolate
Flavours
Packaged Chocolate
Loose Tea

Insight:
Coffee category contributed the highest percentage of overall sales.

7. Order Size Distribution
Studied customer preferences based on drink sizes:
Small
Regular
Large

Insight:
Regular and Large size orders dominated overall sales.
